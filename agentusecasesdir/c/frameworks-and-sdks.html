16 Frameworks & SDKs tools

Agent frameworks give developers building blocks: tool calling, memory, multi-agent orchestration, and tracing. Choice is often driven by language (Python vs. TypeScript) and how much control you want.

What to look for

  • Python or TypeScript
  • Stateful orchestration and retries
  • Model-provider neutrality
