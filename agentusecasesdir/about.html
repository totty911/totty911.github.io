About AgentPlaybooks

AgentPlaybooks is an independent, curated directory of ai agent tools, frameworks & use cases, built for builders and teams adopting AI agents.

How listings are chosen

  • Only products with an official, working website are listed.
  • Every link is checked for a live response before each update. The most recent check was October 19, 2026.
  • The short description is our own summary. The quoted "Vendor says" text is the vendor's own homepage description, copied as-is.
  • We do not list prices because they change often.
  • Sponsored placements are labeled and never replace editorial listings.

Corrections

If a listing is wrong or out of date, use the contact page and it will be fixed.
