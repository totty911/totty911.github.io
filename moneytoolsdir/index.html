FAQ

Is this directory free?

Yes. Browsing is free and listings are free. Vendors can buy a clearly labeled featured spot; that does not change editorial listings.

How are tools chosen?

Tools are added from their official websites. Each link is checked for a working response before publishing, and dead or redirected listings are updated or removed.

Are prices listed?

No. Pricing changes often, so check each vendor's site. Many tools are paid or freemium.

How do I suggest a tool?

Use the contact page. Suggestions are reviewed, not guaranteed.
