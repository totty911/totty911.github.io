5 Mortgage & Housing tools

Mortgage calculators estimate a monthly payment from price, down payment, rate, and term. The better ones include property tax, insurance, PMI, and an amortization schedule.

What to look for

  • Includes taxes, insurance and PMI
  • Shows an amortization schedule
  • Uses your own rate rather than a sponsored one
