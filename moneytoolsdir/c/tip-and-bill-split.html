8 Tip & Bill Split tools

Tip calculators handle a single check; bill-splitting apps track who owes what across a trip or a shared household. Pick by whether you need a quick number or a running balance.

What to look for

  • One-off calculation vs. ongoing group balances
  • Works offline or without an account
  • Handles uneven splits and tax/tip on top
