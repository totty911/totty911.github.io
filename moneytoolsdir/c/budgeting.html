10 Budgeting tools

Budgeting apps range from zero-based (give every dollar a job) to automatic spending trackers. Open-source and self-hosted options keep your data on your own device.

What to look for

  • Method: zero-based, envelopes, or tracking
  • Bank sync vs. manual entry
  • Subscription price and data ownership
