7 Currency & FX tools

Currency converters show exchange rates; the rate you actually pay at a bank or card network is usually different. Mid-market and central-bank reference rates are useful baselines.

What to look for

  • Mid-market vs. retail rate
  • How often rates update
  • Historical charts if you need them
