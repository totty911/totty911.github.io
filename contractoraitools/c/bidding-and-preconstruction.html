5 Bidding & Preconstruction tools

Preconstruction tools help you find projects, send bid invitations, and review contracts before you commit. Some are networks, some are AI assistants that read documents for risk.

What to look for

  • Whether the bid network covers your region and project size
  • How subcontractor invites and responses are tracked
  • Whether AI document review explains why a clause was flagged
