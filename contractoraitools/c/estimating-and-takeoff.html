12 Estimating & Takeoff tools

Takeoff and estimating tools turn plans into quantities and quantities into a price. Newer AI takeoff tools try to count and measure from PDFs automatically, while older desktop tools rely on you clicking areas and runs.

What to look for

  • Which drawing formats it reads (PDF, CAD, scanned sets)
  • Whether takeoff feeds straight into an estimate or proposal
  • Trade fit: general, roofing, electrical, landscaping or paving
